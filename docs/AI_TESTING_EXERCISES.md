# AI-supported testing exercises

AI is a support tool in these exercises, not part of the Quality Shop application. Do not treat generated output as evidence until you have checked it.

## 1. Improve an NUnit test

Ask an AI tool to suggest an improvement to one existing NUnit test. Review whether the name, Arrange–Act–Assert structure, assertions, data, and abstraction level make the intent clearer.

You must verify the suggestion, run or otherwise check it, and describe at least one weakness, error, or uncertainty in the answer.

## 2. Propose a Playwright test

Ask an AI tool to propose a Playwright for .NET test for a user-facing risk that is not yet automated. Check locator robustness, setup, assertions, independence, and diagnostic value.

You must verify the suggestion, run or otherwise check it, and describe at least one weakness, error, or uncertainty in the answer.

## 3. Analyze a k6 result

Give an AI tool selected k6 metrics plus relevant environment and log context. Ask for observations, possible hypotheses, and a next experiment.

You must verify calculations and claims against the source data and describe at least one weakness, error, or uncertainty in the analysis.
